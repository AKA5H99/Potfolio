About

I am a passionate Unity Game Developer focused on creating engaging, polished, and optimized gaming experiences. My journey in game development started with learning the fundamentals of Unity and C#, and has grown through hands-on experience working on different types of games and platforms. I enjoy exploring gameplay mechanics, building systems, improving visuals, and finding ways to make games run smoothly and efficiently.


I have gained industry experience through internships and full-time work, contributing to client and production-level projects across Android, Windows, Linux, and Fire TV/Firestick. My experience includes gameplay implementation, level design, UI, asset integration, backend data integration using JSON, debugging, game polishing, and performance optimization. I have also worked on kiosk and mobile games, where optimization, stability, and overall game quality were important parts of development.


Throughout my experience, I have learned how to work with different development requirements and adapt to different types of projects. I am always interested in learning new technologies, improving my development workflow, and deepening my understanding of game development. I enjoy turning ideas into playable experiences and continuously improving both my technical and creative skills.
